    {R-Max: Approximating Ideal Cache Management}
    {}
    {}
    {
        \begin{cvitems}
            \item Proposed R-Max, an iterative method to approximate the upper limit of ideal prefetching and replacement in L2 cache with realistic bandwidth, capacity and latency constraints.
            \item Implemented and evaluated in ChampSim.
            \item Quantified limits of performance gains from aggressive prefetching and opportune replacement.
            \item Early version published in IEEE Computer Architecture Letters.
            \item Full paper accepted by the 53rd International Symposium on Computer Architecture held in Raleigh, NC, USA from the 27th of June to the 1st of July, 2026.
        \end{cvitems}
    }

    \cventry
    {}
    {Context Switch Optimization}
    {}
    {}
    {
        \begin{cvitems}
            \item Developed a technique to prime microarchitectural states after context switches to mitigate stalls from cold caches in short-timeslice workloads.
            \item Modeled context-switch effects and cache warm-up behavior in ChampSim to evaluate
            performance sensitivity across workloads.
            \item Reclaimed an average 25\% of performance loss caused by 1 ms context switch intervals, evaluated on GAP, XSBench, SPEC CPU2017 and CVP-1 workloads with a 35KiB L2 storage cost.
        \end{cvitems}
    }
